Biography

I am currently a Tenure-Track Associate Professor of School of Intelligence Science and Technology at Nanjing University and a member of Reasoning and Learning Research Group, led by Prof. Yang Gao. I received my Ph.D. degree in School of Computer Science in December 2019 from Nanjing University. I visited the VIStA group from December 2017 to June 2019 at the University of Rochester, under the supervision of Prof. Jiebo Luo.

Research Interests

My research interests encompass,

  • Brain-inspired Artificial Intelligence: I am particularly focused on few-shot concept learning, continual learning, and brain-inspired cognitive computation models, aiming to advance human-level cognitive intelligence.
  • Efficient Generative AI System: My attention is directed towards hardware-software codesign, model compression, LLM quantization, machine learning compilation, and their practical applications in generative AI models for robotics.
  • Multimodal Foundation Models: In addition, I also have a broad interest in multimodal foundation models, model merging, multi-modal reasoning, as well as the deployment and acceleration of large-scale models on edge devices.

    Datasets & Frameworks

    1. LibContinual is a comprehensive library for continual learning, especially for continual image classification. It integrates multiple classic continual methods into a unified framework. This library is friendly for beginners in continual learning with very concise code and clear structure.
    2. LibFewShot is a comprehensive library for few-shot learning, especially for few-shot image classification. It integrates multiple classic FSL methods into a unified framework, including four fine-tuning based methods, six meta-learning based methods, and eight metric-learning based methods.
    3. WebCaricature dataset is developed for caricature recognition. It is a large photograph-caricature dataset consisting of 6042 caricatures and 5974 photographs from 252 persons collected from the web.
